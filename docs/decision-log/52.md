## PART 1 — ⚠ THE RESEARCH ERROR, AND WHY IT IS RECORDED RATHER THAN QUIETLY FIXED

The operator ran the first of those and got back:

The three recommendations, checked against reality:

| pair | sandbox-tradable? | candle data? |
| --- | --- | --- |

**All three were dead, two of them for the reason the operator identified and the

correctly-shaped data that passes every check and that no downstream reader can
distinguish.** Recorded here because a confident wrong answer is the expensive
kind, and because the operator caught it, not the session.

## PART 2 — THE SWEEP: THERE IS NO DIRECTIONAL PAIR TO SELECT

Rather than pick a new subset and risk the same error a second time, **all 243
USD-quoted sandbox symbols were swept** against the real sandbox candle endpoint.

| outcome | count |
| --- | --- |
| **price actually moves** | **9** |
| flat — a single constant close for the whole window | **112** |
| `NoDataFound` | **122** |
| other error | 0 |

And the 9 are not 9 assets. They are **three assets × three quote variants**
(`usd`, `gusd`, `rlusd`) whose metrics are **identical to the last digit** — the
sandbox serves one underlying series per asset regardless of quote:

| asset @ 1m | chg% | rng% | path efficiency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BTC | +1.17 | 3.02 | **0.34** |
| ETH | −0.06 | 1.59 | **0.03** |
| XRP | −0.62 | 1.37 | **0.03** |

The 112 flat pairs are literally constant: **SOLUSD is 1440 candles, 0 with
volume, 1 distinct close**, a flat line at `72.88`. DOGEUSD likewise at `0.09416`.
Neither tracks production.

`NoDataFound` was **verified as real rather than assumed**: every failing symbol
was re-fetched with 3-second gaps and returned
`{"result":"error","reason":"NoDataFound","message":"Candles are empty for given symbol or time_frame"}`.
That check exists because the failures were interspersed with successes, which is
the signature of rate limiting, and reporting rate limiting as "no data" would
have been a second confident wrong answer.

**⚠ THE CONCLUSION IS THAT THE ORIGINAL PLAN WAS UNACHIEVABLE.** The task as
framed — pick better pairs — cannot work here. The entire usable universe of this
venue is BTC, ETH and XRP, and **all three are range-bound at 1m**. BTCUSD's 0.34
is the *highest* path efficiency available, and BTCUSD is the pair that had
already returned `grid` eleven times.

**This also reframes the 11/11 grid result.** It was never evidence of a model
bias or a broken DCA branch. `VERIFIED_INTERVALS` is `["1m"]` and Gemini's
`/v2/candles` returns a fixed recent window with **no time-range parameter**, so
`since` can only ever *narrow* it. Every strategy judgement this project has ever
made was made over **24 hours of 1-minute candles**, and over 24 hours on this
data the price genuinely does oscillate inside a band — which is the literal
wording of grid's fit criterion in `strategyDefinitions()`. **The model was
reading the data correctly. The window was the constraint.**

### The spacing verification, run as research before anything was edited

`VERIFIED_INTERVALS`' docblock sets the bar in its own words: *"an interval
belongs here only once someone has actually read that venue's candles at that
timeframe and confirmed the duration is what it claims."* That was done, against
the real sandbox, by measuring every consecutive candle gap:

| Gemini timeframe | measured spacing | `GEMINI_TIMEFRAMES` `ms` | verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `1hr` | **60 min × 1462 gaps** | `3_600_000` | matches |
| `6hr` | **360 min × 364 gaps** (one 720-min gap, a single missing candle) | `21_600_000` | matches |
| `1day` | **1440 min × 109 gaps** | `86_400_000` | matches |

### ⚠ THE BAR WAS MET FOR ONE VENUE AND NOT FOR TWO OTHERS, AND THE PROBE SAID SO IN ITS OWN COMMENT

This is the part most worth preserving, because it is the part a future permanent
widening must not inherit as though it were settled:

- **MET for Gemini sandbox** — the only venue the probe touched.
- **NOT MET for Gemini production** — never read at this timeframe.
- **NOT MET for Binance at all.** `VERIFIED_INTERVALS` is a **global** constant,
  but the docblock it quotes demands verification *"PER EXCHANGE BEFORE FIRST
  USE."* Adding `"1h"` silently asserts it for Binance too. Binance passes
  intervals through unchanged so the mis-mapping failure mode is structurally
  absent there — **but "structurally absent" is an argument, and the bar demands
  a reading.**

**Sufficient for a temporary probe against `gemini-main`. NOT sufficient for a
permanent widening.** Whether `1h` becomes a first-class verified interval is a
**separate decision, explicitly not made here**, and it carries a real cost of its
own: it weakens the 21.7 depth-limit question entry 29 left open.

### ⚠ THE SPELLING TRAP, RECORDED BECAUSE IT ALMOST LANDED

The brief for this step said add `"1hr"`. **`"1hr"` is Gemini's WIRE spelling
only**, produced by `GEMINI_TIMEFRAMES`; the canonical literal is **`"1h"`** and
`"1hr"` is not a `CandleInterval` and would not typecheck. The API query parameter
is `interval=1h`. This is precisely the venue-spelling confusion
`VERIFIED_INTERVALS`' own warning is about, arriving in the instructions to widen
it.

## PART 5 — TWO CITATION OBSERVATIONS. NEITHER IS A BUG.

Recorded because entry 43 asked for exactly this kind of watching, and entry 44
established the precedent of logging a frequency observation rather than acting
on it.

**1. `autoRestart` rests only on `assessment.strategy` — the first (c)-only field
ever seen on a DCA proposal.** Entry 41 first flagged the pattern, entry 43
declined to enforce anything about it, entry 44 saw three (c)-only fields at once
on a grid proposal. **This is the same pattern, now confirmed to occur on the DCA
branch too** — which is mildly reassuring rather than alarming: it means the
behaviour is a property of the Derive stage, not of one strategy's prompt.
**None of entry 43's reopening conditions is met.** No fourth citation class
appeared, the operator reviewed the value and did not judge it unsound, and it
was identified immediately from the field-level warning.

**2. `sellOnStopLoss` cites `context.gathered_at` — a timestamp that does not
substantively justify a boolean.** The **value is correct** (`false`, the only
value `validateDcaParams` accepts, and the prompt demands it in terms), and the
citation is a real id the run genuinely emitted, so nothing fails and nothing is
mis-rendered. But *"when this stage's extra reads ran"* is not evidence for *"do
not sell on a stop-loss halt"* — the real justification is a system constraint
stated in the prompt, and **there is no evidence id for a system constraint.**

**This is a citation-QUALITY note, not a citation-CLASS one**, and the distinction
matters. Entry 43's machinery classifies what a citation *points at*; it has never
claimed to judge whether pointing there was *apt*. Entry 44 said so directly:
*"Classifying a citation says what a value rests on; it does not say whether
resting on it was wise."* **This is the first concrete instance of that limit
showing up on real data** — a citation that is well-formed, correctly classified
as (a), rendered correctly, and still not much of a reason. **No mechanism is
proposed and none should be**, because judging aptness is judging model prose by
meaning, which entry 43 rejected as option C.
